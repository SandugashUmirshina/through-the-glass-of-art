Mission

Art is never just art

Through the Glass of Art is a free platform that teaches economics and art side by side. My mission is to spread this kind of education internationally, especially to youth in underrepresented communities who don't usually get access to it.

I want to give youth digital access to knowledge in economics and finance through art, while also building a real appreciation for art itself.

About

How it works

Click on a painting, a ballet, or a piece of literature, and learn the financial history behind it: the artist, the country, and the period it came from. Everything on the site is free, with no sign-up and no ads.

Why This Exists

History and economics belong together

Art and economics are usually taught apart, even though they were never really separate. I started this because I believe understanding both together is how art should be taught, and that knowledge should be available to any student, anywhere, for free.

Goals

What we're working toward

Teach the financial history behind art, not just the art itself.

Stay free and ad-free, always.

Reach youth internationally, with real focus on underrepresented communities.

Cover painting, dance, and literature, not just one discipline.

Through the Glass of Art — a free educational project. No ads, no accounts, no cost.
