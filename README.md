# Through the Glass of Art

A free platform that teaches economics and financial history through art. Click on a painting, ballet, or opera and learn the real economic story behind the artist, country, and period it came from.

**Live site:** https://through-the-glass-of-art.vercel.app

## About

This project connects art history to economics, something rarely taught together despite being deeply intertwined. Each piece is researched, written, and checked for public domain licensing individually.

**Volume I: Standard** — Monetary policy and currency, six pieces from Song Dynasty China to the Dutch tulip bubble.

**Volume II: Labor and Industry** — Work, class, and industrialization, currently in progress.

Built for students internationally, with a focus on underrepresented communities who often lack access to resources like this. No sign-up, no ads, no cost.
